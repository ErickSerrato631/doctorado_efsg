\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Physical interpretation of the results}
Allee thresholds act as energy-like barriers; nonreciprocity skews flows and transient routes. Control via $\mu$ or $u$ can steer trajectories below the Allee threshold to favor extinction or containment.

\subsection{Comparison with previous models}
Contrast with reciprocal Lotka--Volterra and immune--tumor ODEs lacking Allee terms; highlight how bifurcations and pattern scales change once asymmetry and control are introduced. Emphasize that weak vs strong Allee shifts the ease of containment and pattern sizes.

\subsection{Implications and limitations}
Current scans show only unstable equilibria with $i^*>0$ in sampled ranges; stability may emerge for lower $r_c$ or higher $rd,\delta$. Spatial results are short-time ($T\sim2$); longer horizons, heterogeneous domains, and optimized $u(\mathbf{x},t)$ protocols remain open. Control acts as a selector of pattern scale; strong Allee caps domain growth, while $\mu=1$ smooths fragmentation.
